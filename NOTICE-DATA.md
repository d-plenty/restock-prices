# About the price data

The files in `latest/` and `history/` are prices and product details that were publicly shown on the
websites of Coles, Woolworths, Aldi and Costco Australia, collected about once a week by the collector in
`collector/`.

- **Not affiliated.** This project isn't connected with, endorsed by or sponsored by Coles, Woolworths, Aldi
  or Costco. Their names, product names, brands and pictures belong to them. Picture links point at the
  shops' own picture servers.
- **No licence is given for the data.** The GPL-3.0 licence in this repository covers the collector's code
  only. The data is shared as is, for personal use, such as the Restock app working out whether a special is
  a real special.
- **Each shop's website terms apply.** Some shops' terms don't allow collecting their listings with
  automated tools (Costco's say so outright). Check them before you use this data for anything else.
- **Prices can be wrong or out of date.** They're online prices on the day collected, not your local shelf
  price. Costco's online prices include delivery.
- **No personal information.** No accounts are signed in to, and nothing about any shopper is collected.

If you're one of these shops and want something removed, open an issue.
